HOW TO DECIDE

A record of one of the ten kinds is about ONE PERSON'S OWN AFFAIRS, and the text shows the particulars that make it theirs: their name together with a passenger or booking reference, an account or card number or a balance, a patient or member number or a diagnosis, treatment or vaccine given to them, a passport, licence or identity number, a login or a code, or a signature line that binds them. Ask first: whose particulars does this text show? If the answer is nobody's, the file is none of the ten kinds.

These are NOT records, whatever words they use: a paper, an article, a lecture, a textbook page or a course assignment about a disease, a bank, a law or a market; a dataset or a table of many rows about many people or many places; a form that is blank or is a template; a flyer, a poster, a syllabus, a schedule or a menu; an exercise that presents an invented statement, invoice or patient; a list of names with no number, diagnosis or booking beside them; a photograph or a graphic with no such particulars. A word like "patient", "hypertension", "invoice", "balance", "passport" or "will" appearing in the text is not a record of anything; the record is the document that carries a named person's own particulars.

Ask, for each kind, whether the released text shows a person's own record of that kind. If exactly one does, that is your answer, and you cite the text that shows it: the span that carries the person's name or the particular, not a topic word. If more than one does, answer the one the file most is, and put the others in "alternatives". If none does, answer "none_of_these".
