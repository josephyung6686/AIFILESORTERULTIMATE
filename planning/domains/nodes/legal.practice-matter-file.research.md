# Research memo — `legal.practice-matter-file`

Date: 2026-08-22
Output: `planning/domains/nodes/legal.practice-matter-file.json`
Roster row: template on the fieldless `legal` schema, `parent_id: null`, placeholder launch

## Result

Accept the node as a deliberately shallow professional template. Its distinct job is to recognize the practitioner side of one representation or matter and keep the resulting packet protected. It does not recreate a practice-area taxonomy, infer legal status, or authorize a client-named filesystem tree.

The node passes the template test even though its dimensions remain empty. The broad Legal default covers personal legal records and generic legal material. This row instead needs practitioner-role evidence, representation and intake anchors, matter-system provenance, filing-service notices, internal work-product structure, and cross-matter isolation. Its default privacy posture is also materially stricter because the existence, client, parties, subject, strategy, and packet membership can disclose a professional relationship. Those differences affect activation, grouping, review, and residual routing rather than fields.

## External artifact research

The external pass used primary professional or court sources only. It establishes that the proposed artifact shapes and role seams occur in real practice. It does not import legal rules into the node.

- [United States Courts — Electronic Filing CM/ECF](https://www.uscourts.gov/court-records/electronic-filing-cm-ecf) identifies pleadings, motions, petitions, case information, and docket information as ordinary electronic court-record artifacts. This supports filed-document and docket fixtures, not a conclusion that a downloaded filing belongs to the holder.
- [United States Courts — CM/ECF frequently asked questions](https://www.uscourts.gov/court-records/file-a-case-cm-ecf/faqs-case-management-electronic-case-files-cm-ecf) describes docket sheets, filed documents, and electronic notices containing links to filed material. This supports the docket and electronic-filing-notice recognition shapes.
- [American Bar Association — Model Rule 1.5](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_5_fees/) discusses communication of representation scope and fee basis. This supports engagement-record sections as observable structure only.
- [American Bar Association — Model Rule 1.6](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/) treats information relating to a representation as a confidentiality concern. This supports a cautious local-first boundary, not a universal handling class or a privilege decision.
- [State Bar of California — Formal Opinion 1994-134](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/sites/default/files/portals/0/documents/ethics/Opinions/1994-134.htm) describes a potential client-file inventory that includes pleadings, correspondence, investigative and research reports, discovery, witness material, and strategy material. This supports breadth of the file set. The opinion is advisory and jurisdiction-specific.
- [Solicitors Regulation Authority — Confidentiality of client information](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/confidentiality-client-information/) addresses client and matter information, redaction, limited disclosure, third parties, and cloud services. This supports minimization and cross-matter caution, not a product-level legal conclusion.
- [Solicitors Regulation Authority — Client and matter risk assessments](https://news.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/client-and-matter-risk-assessments/) describes practitioner-side client and matter assessment workflows. This supports the assessment fixture and its labelled slots.
- [Solicitors Regulation Authority — Conflicts of interest](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/conflicts-interest/) confirms that client and matter role distinctions are operationally meaningful in legal-practice workflows. It does not authorize the product to decide whether a conflict exists.

These sources span a United States federal court system, model professional rules, one United States state-bar advisory opinion, and an England and Wales regulator. They are deliberately used only for artifact existence, observable roles, and privacy seams. The node does not treat them as one universal rule set and derives no retention period, filing deadline, substantive advice, jurisdiction rule, privilege status, disclosure right, or professional-compliance outcome from them.

## Node-test analysis

Three designs were considered.

1. Keep a distinct, fieldless professional template. This is the roster-approved result. It captures representation workflow, practitioner-side anchors, matter-packet grouping, and confidentiality boundaries while staying within J-IND.
2. Fold all artifacts into the broad Legal default. This would lose the decisive holder-role distinction, blur personal legal records with client matters, and omit the cross-matter privacy firewall.
3. Add matter, client, party, practice area, phase, tribunal, jurisdiction, document kind, and similar fields. This would violate D1, PR-6, canonical-field ownership, and the J-IND limit. It would also recreate the rejected overnight expansion into many legal siblings.

The accepted design is not distinguished by filenames, extensions, court vocabulary, or work types. It is distinguished by a coherent professional situation with observable roles and workflows. Therefore it is a valid template rather than a disguised file-kind node.

## Rejected or deferred artifacts

- A practice-management database, document-management database, or email account is a source system, not one file node. A bounded export with a readable manifest is represented; live-system ingestion remains a later connector and security decision.
- Contact exports and address books are not activated as matter files merely because they contain practitioner, client, court, expert, or witness names. Relationship roles require evidence in a matter workflow.
- Raw medical, identity, financial, photographic, or audiovisual evidence retains its own schema evidence. Packet membership cannot erase or copy those facts.
- Public statutes, cases, articles, practice guides, and precedents remain reading or research material unless an exact accepted matter reference establishes membership.
- Password-protected archives, malformed productions, and unknown binaries are represented without forced inspection.
- Practice-area lists, procedural stages, transaction categories, jurisdictions, tribunals, party taxonomies, and document taxonomies are deferred. Adding them here would turn a gist placeholder into the industry-depth catalogue J-IND forbids in this round.

## Fields and dimensions

`fields: []`, `proposed_fields: []`, and `template.dimension_order: []` are intentional. `time_first` is false.

The following candidates were rejected:

- `client` and `our_firm` are canonical engagement-role keys, but the Legal schema does not reference them. They also cannot represent every party, counsel, or professional role safely.
- `project` and `artifact_type` are scoped to Research and Code.
- `institution` and `record_type` are scoped to Finance.
- `purpose` remains scoped to College Applications under the current canonical record.
- `matter_id`, `case_number`, `counsel_role`, `party`, `counterparty`, `practice_area`, `phase`, `tribunal`, `document_kind`, and similar candidates are not canonical Legal fields. This row does not mint them.
- `jurisdiction` is explicitly unavailable as a field or dimension under the current decision brief.

A chronological first level would scatter one representation because filing time, event time, document date, receipt time, and filesystem creation time have different meanings. If Legal fields are ratified later, an organizing matter anchor would normally precede function or time, but that remains a future design decision rather than a serialized placeholder dimension.

The present physical recommendation is one shallow, redacted, user-approved packet. A visible client or matter branch can itself disclose that a representation exists, so no automatic client-named or matter-named depth is proposed.

## Recognition boundary

Strong evidence combines labelled roles and a workflow:

- engagement or representation record plus client and practitioner blocks;
- client-and-matter intake, review, or assessment structure;
- court filing plus counsel role tied to the holder;
- court-system notice delivered to the holder practitioner account;
- docket or discovery artifact linked by an exact filed-document, proceeding, production, or matter reference;
- internal analysis or strategy structure plus practitioner-team producer marker;
- client or counsel communication crossing an explicit role boundary;
- matter billing or calendar structure plus a separately evidenced representation;
- matter-system export manifest containing coherent professional families.

Weak evidence remains weak even in combination when it lacks the role anchor. Legal words, names, captions, identifiers, dates, public availability, folder paths, metadata, shared download sessions, semantic similarity, and confidentiality legends do not activate this row alone. A filename can retrieve a candidate for local review but cannot create a client or matter fact.

The holder-role question is essential. Identical bytes may be a practitioner client file, an individual party record, a public court download, a consulting engagement artifact, or a Finance record. The classifier must interpret explicit roles locally or abstain.

## Privacy and legal-status boundary

The packet is client-confidential by default as a catalogue posture, but that phrase is not serialized as a legal conclusion or handling class. The node records only `potentially_sensitive`.

It must never infer:

- attorney-client privilege, work-product protection, sealing, a protective order, waiver, or disclosure authority;
- whether a public filing makes the local file or neighboring packet low sensitivity;
- whether a client instruction constitutes informed consent or legal authority;
- whether a filing, agreement, signature, service event, deadline, or legal position is valid, effective, current, satisfied, missed, or correct;
- which retention, deletion, transfer, or production duty applies;
- which jurisdiction or professional rule controls.

Recognition and grouping should occur locally from the minimum necessary excerpt or manifest. Full dossiers, addresses, identity records, message bodies, strategy, and evidence should not be placed into remote prompts by default. Cross-matter semantic similarity is suppressed because names, issues, authorities, experts, and counterparties may recur without authorizing a relationship. Exact references can support a reviewable candidate edge, but they do not copy the anchor label onto a member.

## Edges and role boundaries

Three mutex collisions are authored.

- `legal.personal-legal-matters` — the same court and agreement artifacts occur on both sides. The deciding evidence is practitioner-side representation workflow versus the holder as a personal party with no practitioner anchor.
- `career.consulting-client-engagement` — engagement letters, statements of work, fees, confidentiality, signatures, correspondence, and deliverables overlap. Explicit counsel, representation, legal-services, tribunal, matter-system, or legal-work-product structure supports this row; consulting prepared-for and prepared-by roles with milestones and acceptance support Career.
- `finance.small-business-bookkeeping` — time entries, invoices, expenses, budgets, and ledgers may be both operational financial evidence and matter members. Finance owns the financial slots; an exact representation reference supports packet membership without converting them into Legal facts.

`also_holds_with` remains empty because this template cannot author schema-level coactivation and Legal exposes no role field to split. The fixtures instead record independent `also_schema` cases for Finance, Identity, and Photos. `role_split` is empty for the same fieldless reason.

The holder-role ambiguity is already expressed against `legal.personal-legal-matters` at J-IND gist depth. If landed sibling research reveals a true reciprocal same-evidence mutex, R1c can add it without expanding this row into a legal taxonomy.
- `identity.core-documents` is a coactivation case for a passport or identity copy inside a matter, not a mutex.
- `photos.screenshot-captures` is a coactivation case for an e-filing or portal screen capture, not a mutex.
- `medical.personal-health-records` can coexist with Legal when a medical record is evidence. Neither schema should erase the other.
- `research.reading-library` is not a same-evidence mutex. A public opinion with no accepted purpose falls to Reading Inbox; a copy explicitly referenced by active research or a matter can retain that membership.
- Code is not activated merely by native production formats, e-discovery exports, or technical exhibits. It needs its own repository or software evidence.
- `finance.loans-mortgage` may coexist with transaction or closing material. The bookkeeping collision already captures the genuinely confusable invoice, time, expense, and ledger evidence.

## Grouping without copied facts

Candidate groups are bounded by an explicit representation anchor and exact matter, filing, production, transcript, exhibit, version, or export reference. The group can include sparse notes, calendar events, emails, invoices, identity records, media, and archives. Membership does not create client, matter, party, date, topic, confidentiality, or legal-status facts on the member.

An archive manifest may be inspected without unpacking. A matter export remains shallow when its internal access boundaries are unknown. No packet joins another matter merely through party names, subjects, citations, experts, counsel, dates, or semantic similarity.

## Residual routing

- Protected Records is the principal fallback for recognized engagement material, filings, strategy, correspondence, evidence, identity copies, and matter packets when no accepted professional group is active.
- Review Later receives readable professional-looking artifacts whose holder role or workflow is unresolved, including a consulting-shaped engagement without a legal anchor.
- Unsupported or Encrypted receives inaccessible archives, malformed productions, practice-system binaries, and unknown formats without manufacturing purpose from their names.
- Reading Inbox receives public opinions, articles, reports, guides, and precedent copies with no accepted matter or research association.
- Temporary Screenshots receives a positively evidenced screen capture when OCR does not establish an accepted matter or neighboring workflow.

No residual is treated as a schema fact or permanent destination mandate.

## J-IND limitations and tensions

- The roster asks for one comprehensive professional-world placeholder while D1 and PR-6 prohibit Legal fields. The result can be deep in evidence boundaries but must remain shallow in structure.
- A template normally recommends dimensions, yet no destination-eligible Legal field exists. An empty order is the only contract-compliant recommendation.
- Client-confidential-by-default is necessary for safety, but the classifier cannot decide privilege, work product, sealing, waiver, consent, or disclosure rights.
- Court material may be public while the holder relationship, search history, local annotations, surrounding correspondence, and packet membership remain sensitive.
- Exact matter references are useful for grouping, but exposing them in branch labels may itself reveal a representation.
- Professional legal practice varies by jurisdiction and work type. This pass recognizes common artifact structures and deliberately avoids substantive or jurisdiction-specific logic.

## NEEDS-JOSEPH

1. If D1 or PR-6 changes, decide which Legal fields may exist at all. Candidate concepts include a matter identifier, client, holder firm, party, counterparty, counsel role, practice area, tribunal, phase, document kind, and operative date. This row proposes none.
2. Decide whether any client-named or matter-named physical branch is acceptable, or whether all professional packets must keep redacted display labels and local-only aliases.
3. Decide how P9 records exact matter, filing, production, transcript, version, and manifest membership without copying anchor facts, and how cross-matter retrieval barriers are represented.
4. Let P7 and explicit user policy own the distinctions among public court material, client confidentiality, privilege, work product, sealing, protective orders, and disclosure consent. This catalogue must remain observational and abstaining.

## Final recommendation

Keep `legal.practice-matter-file` as a placeholder template with no fields, no dimensions, no parent activation, no schema coactivation edge, and no time-first hierarchy. Use the practitioner-side representation workflow as the recognition discriminator, preserve all legal-status uncertainty, group only through exact bounded anchors, and route unmatched material conservatively.
