## Official artifact research

Only first-party support or developer documentation was used for external artifact checking. The
sources establish what users can actually download and the structures those records expose. They
were not used to encode medical interpretation, legal obligations, retention periods, or
jurisdiction-specific rules.

- Apple documents an all-data Health export in XML on its
  [Health data sharing guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/share-your-health-data-iph5ede58c3d/26/ios/26).
  The same guide distinguishes app- and device-written fitness data from provider health records,
  which supports the collision with `medical.personal-health-records`.
- Apple separately documents a wearable ECG recording and the ability to export a PDF on its
  [ECG support page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120278). This establishes the
  device-generated report fixture without licensing the catalogue to interpret the result.
- Google Health documents a complete Fitbit account archive, request and completion emails,
  family-account folders, selectable recent exports, and TCX workout exports on its
  [data export page](https://support.google.com/googlehealth/answer/14236615?hl=en).
- Garmin documents original activity files, TCX and GPX variants, activity and report CSVs,
  whole-account exports, and wellness FIT files delivered in a ZIP on its
  [Connect export guide](https://support.garmin.com/en-GB/?faq=W1TvTPW8JZ6LfJSfK512Q8).
- Oura documents downloadable account data, export-request completion email, and printable sleep,
  movement, and cycle reports on its
  [export and sharing guide](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025441594-Export-Share-Your-Oura-Data).
- Strava documents original FIT files, TCX records with heart rate and cadence, GPX files with
  coordinates and timestamps, and a bulk account archive on its
  [bulk export guide](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401919-exporting-your-data-and-bulk-export).
- Dexcom documents downloadable PDF reports and a CSV export containing raw glucose values,
  calibration values, and events in its
  [Clarity home user guide](https://productstore.clarity.dexcom.com/Documentation/en/Dexcom_Clarity_User_Guide_Home_User.pdf).
- Android Health Connect documents the generic structure behind many app records: record type,
  start and end time, unit-bearing values, series samples, recording method, and device
  provenance on its [structured data guide](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/write-data).
  Those are structural roles, not a detector vocabulary copied into the node.
- Samsung distinguishes downloaded provider records and a generated clinical-record PDF on its
  [health records guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10003847/). That is the
  provider-issued side of the sibling collision rather than evidence that all health-app files
  are wearable telemetry.

The comparison matters because no one extension owns this situation. A real corpus can contain a
whole-account archive, XML, JSON, CSV, FIT, TCX, GPX, PDF, screenshot, and email from one export
workflow. The stable signal is the role and structure of the data plus explicit export provenance,
not a vendor name, suffix, or one metric term.

## Node test and alternatives

Three approaches were considered.

1. Refuse the row and rely on the Medical schema default. This would preserve the required empty
   fields and dimensions, but it would collapse a recognizable account-export lifecycle into the
   much broader clinical detector. The schema default covers lab tables, provider documents,
   prescriptions, claims, calendars, and general health-app archives. It does not carry the
   concrete boundaries among longitudinal telemetry, activity containers, export indices,
   account emails, Research datasets, and social-platform takeouts.
2. Treat the data as Photos, Research, Code, or a new field-bearing Medical schema. That fails in
   several ways. A GPX route is not a photo merely because it has a location; JSON and XML are
   source types, not Code facts; a personal account export is not a Research dataset without a
   project or codebook; and D1 plus PR-6 forbids Medical field rows.
3. Keep a distinct field-less Medical safety template whose value is precise export recognition,
   packet grouping, collision boundaries, and bulk privacy. This is the implemented approach. It
   differs materially from the schema default despite sharing the empty dimension surface, so it
   passes the template node test.

The assignment fixes this roster row and asks for its R1b design. That is the approved boundary for
the third approach; the two-file ownership rule prevents a separate design document.

## Fields and dimensions

`fields`, `proposed_fields`, and `dimension_order` are all empty by contract and by safety.

The inherited Medical field set is empty under D1 and PR-6. Tempting concepts were all rejected:

- `export_source` is only a proposal recorded by another template; it is not a canonical key, and
  D1 forbids using this row to create Medical fields.
- device, metric family, subject, measurement interval, report kind, activity, condition, and
  account are not canonical Medical keys.
- `record_type` and `institution` are Finance-scoped and cannot silently become Medical fields.
- `artifact_type`, `project`, and `stage` remain Research facts on independently evidenced study
  material.
- `capture_year`, `event`, and `location` are Photos facts. A GPX point or wearable timestamp is
  not capture evidence.
- `purpose` remains College-applications-scoped under PR-1.
- `jurisdiction` is a value and never a field or dimension under D4.

The empty dimension order follows mechanically because a template may branch only on fields its
schema declares. Privacy independently reaches the same answer. App, device, metric, sleep,
glucose, ECG, pregnancy, location, and date-range labels disclose the material through the
filesystem namespace. The recommendation is one shallow protected packet or representation,
preserving the user-approved export family. It is not time-first because measurement time, export
time, and file creation time are distinct, and splitting one archive by any of them destroys its
workflow coherence.

The `work_types` list describes artifact values for recognition and review. It does not imply a
legal `work_type` Medical fact and creates no child nodes.

## Recognition and privacy boundary

The deterministic families are structural:

- account-export manifest plus several health-shaped member families;
- repeated XML records with type, source, unit, time, and value roles;
- per-metric tables with an explicit export anchor and source-device provenance;
- an approved activity parser yielding activity, track, and sensor roles;
- device-generated ECG or trend reports with explicit exporter provenance;
- sensor exports with raw reading, calibration, and event regions;
- export indices and explicit member references;
- multipart packages joined by an export identifier rather than similar names.

Every shortcut has a matching `never_alone`: metric vocabulary, vendor name, source type,
extension, filename, generic time-series columns, GPS, chart shape, export email, folder, session,
missing EXIF, archive size, account token, device identifier, and an encrypted name.

Bulk sensitivity is the defining privacy difference. A long collection of individually ordinary
steps, timestamps, routes, and device records can reveal routine, absence, home and work
locations, sleep, exercise, health events, and relationships. Therefore:

- Medical protection precedes every model and placement path.
- Raw XML, JSON, CSV, FIT, TCX, GPX, report content, OCR, manifests, paths, identifiers, hashes,
  GPS, group memberships, and values remain local.
- Complete exports are never used as cloud dossiers. Local interpretation or abstention is the
  default for every `needs_llm` case.
- Filenames, archive member lists, thumbnails, metric labels, and summaries are redacted in shared
  UI under user policy.
- Archives are inspected by manifest and never unpacked to disk during the normal scan.
- `sensitivity` is only `potentially_sensitive`. No P7 handling class, alias, ranking, detector
  outcome, or threshold is authored.

## Edges and neighbor decisions

`collides_with` contains only same-kind roster templates.

- `medical.personal-health-records` — provider-issued clinical documents and personal device
  telemetry share XML, CSV, PDF, portal, and app shapes. Issuer and function discriminate them.
- `photos.social-media-export` — both are whole-account bundles with JSON, XML, media, and account
  metadata. Health record types and sensor provenance differ from per-media sidecars, posts,
  albums, contacts, and conversations.
- `research.dataset-analysis` — reciprocal with the landed row. An app-generated measurement CSV
  needs a project identifier, codebook, or analysis relationship to take the Research side.

Edges deliberately not written:

- `photos.screenshot-captures` — a health dashboard capture genuinely carries both Photos capture
  evidence and Medical content. That is co-activation, recorded as `also_schema: photos`, not a
  template mutex.
- `photos.camera-events` and `travel.trip-photos` — GPX coordinates and activity times are not
  camera facts or a photo event. File format and location values do not create a template edge.
- `photos.scanned-documents` — a scanned provider document already resolves through the
  personal-health-record collision. A device report exported directly as PDF is not a scan.
- `code.software-project` and `code.notebooks-experiments` — XML, JSON, and binary structure are
  source routing evidence. Repository or package structure is what activates Code.
- `finance.insurance-healthcare` — a reimbursement file can legitimately carry Finance facts and
  Medical protection on disjoint evidence, but an account telemetry export has no insurance
  reading. No mutex is needed.
- `identity.credentials-passwords` — an export email can contain a login control or temporary
  download link, but this node stores no credential. A credential export activates Identity on
  its own evidence.
- `research.ethics-compliance` — participant consent and study governance are distinct artifacts.
  The dataset collision already covers the actual telemetry input.

`also_holds_with` is empty because CONNECTION restricts it to schema pairs. Real co-activation is
shown in `file_examples[].also_schema`. `role_split` is empty because it joins canonical fields and
Medical declares none. `parent_id` is `null` because R1b never authors browse shelving.
`shares_field` is derived-only and is not serialized.

The `research.dataset-analysis` and `medical.personal-health-records` pairs are already reciprocal.
The collision toward `photos.social-media-export` remains one-way until that row is revisited or
R1c reconciles it. No neighbor was modified.

## Residuals

- `Protected Records` is the primary home for a recognized but ungrouped export, device report,
  activity trace, or sensor table.
- `Unsupported or Encrypted` represents an unreadable export or unsupported binary without forcing
  it open or inferring purpose from its name.
- `Review Later` catches a readable app or sensor export whose supported domain remains unclear.
- `Temporary Screenshots` catches a positively identified screen capture whose OCR does not
  establish personal health material.
- `Reading Inbox` catches public guides and report samples with no personal account or project.

The latter four describe inactive or unreadable outcomes. Once Medical evidence is established,
the protected path dominates; a recognized health export is not sent to a generic review folder.

## Binding tensions carried forward

1. The Medical schema already lists a generic health-application export archive as one detector.
   This template is still non-duplicate because it adds real account-export formats, explicit
   member-link rules, report and email workflow, bulk privacy, and concrete template collisions.
   If R1c decides that specificity is insufficient, refusal is preferable to preserving a hollow
   row.
2. The prompt asks templates to recommend dimensions, while D1 and PR-6 make every Medical
   dimension illegal. The binding contract wins and the order is empty.
3. The roster hint calls each datum superficially trivial, but the catalogue has no legal field
   for the aggregate risk. That risk stays in privacy rules and does not become a new fact.
4. FIT and other proprietary device formats are real artifacts, but `00` requires unsupported
   formats to remain metadata-only until a dedicated extractor is explicitly approved. The node
   names no parser implementation and infers nothing from an extension.
5. Exact manifest references can support packet membership, but the grouping firewall forbids
   label propagation. No export-index term becomes a member fact.
6. D2 makes P7 classification authoritative and its detector injected and unwritten. This node
   provides recognition and the two-valued catalogue sensitivity posture only; it does not
   restate P7 vocabulary.
