# creative.deliverable-handoff — full J-DEPTH research memo

**Depth:** FULL R1b / J-DEPTH (not gist).  
**Verdict:** KEEP, narrowly.  
**Node test:** survives on a detection difference and a privacy/custody difference; it does not survive on format, a terminal project state, or dimensions.  
**Defining lifecycle:** enumerated package → identified receipt or exact-version acceptance → rights or custody conditions attached to those same members.  
**Schema contract:** `fields: []`, `dimension_order: []`, placeholder launch.  
**Collision fixture:** `Project Phoenix Closure and Benefits Review.pdf` is terminal project evidence and is not a creative handoff.  

## Scope and conclusion

The roster label is dangerously close to three things that are already represented elsewhere: an `artifact_type` value such as export, a `stage` value such as delivered, and the terminal state of a bounded project. None of those licenses a template. A `.zip` is a container; `FINAL` is free text; “accepted” can approve a brief, a purchase, a milestone, or an invoice. If the row meant only “the files actually given to a client,” it should be refused because the creative schema already names “an export whose whole reason for existing is that someone else receives it,” and `creative.client-engagement` already recognises “a DELIVERY MANIFEST or handoff note listing final creative assets, formats or variants and a recipient, paired with the named exported files; one export alone is not a delivery structure.”

The row survives only after tightening the object. The retained situation is a chain of records that makes four propositions observable without turning them into schema facts: what exact bytes constituted the package; which receiver-side event acknowledged or accepted which revision; what use conditions or third-party restrictions travel with those bytes; and who has custody of editable sources or physical masters after transfer. The package is not merely “finished.” It crosses an organisational boundary and leaves a reciprocal sender/receiver trace plus a rights or custody tail. This is a narrower terminal dossier inside a creative lifecycle, not a synonym for the lifecycle.

That distinction is inferential, not a new quote from the product design. The sole `design_cite` is the existing exact product-design span used by the creative schema: “covering common organizational situations such as academic programs, university applications, recruiting processes, client engagements, research workflows, financial records, travel, legal matters, creative projects, software repositories, personal administration, and photo collections”. All other claims below are observations about named artifacts or explicitly marked boundary arguments.

## Sources and comparison set

I used the stamped assignment generated by `planning/domains/dispatch/make_prompt.py creative.deliverable-handoff`, the standing `RESEARCH-BRIEF.md`, and the landed node records named in the charge: `creative.json`, `creative.client-engagement.json`, `business_operations.project-delivery.json`, `business_operations.contract-administration.json`, and `logistics.json`. I also checked the roster existence of `creative.licensing-rights`, `logistics.shipment`, `legal`, and `finance` before emitting edges. The shallow business rows were used for key shape and reciprocal boundary idiom, not as a depth target.

The creative schema supplies the decisive baseline. It already recognises “a DELIVERY or HANDOFF structure: an export set whose members share one stem across format and size variants (print/web, CMYK/RGB, @1x/@2x/@3x, multiple aspect ratios or market cuts) accompanied by a note listing what is enclosed. The multiplicity IS the signal; a single .png is nothing.” Therefore export multiplicity plus a note is the schema default and cannot distinguish this template. It also already lists “one delivery or handoff set, recognised by a shared stem across format and size variants” as a grouping reason. This memo does not claim either as novel.

`creative.client-engagement` is closer still. It owns the commissioner-maker relationship from request and scope through review and delivery, including an acceptance record and a manifest. The retained handoff row begins where that broad relationship stops being the object: it asks whether exact package members can be traced through receiver acknowledgement or artifact-specific acceptance and onward into usage restrictions or custody duties. A full engagement can exist without that closure chain; a technical handoff to a printer or publisher can have the chain even when the recipient is not the commissioning client.

`business_operations.project-delivery` owns governance of a bounded effort. Its natural objects are charter, plan, status, risk, issue, dependency, change, closure, lessons and benefits. A creative package can be one project deliverable, but a sponsor signing project closure does not tell us which PNGs, masters, fonts, or source files crossed the boundary, who received them, or on what rights and custody conditions.

`business_operations.contract-administration` owns the executed instrument's running apparatus: registers, clause-referenced obligations, notices, renewals, variations, remedies and service-level administration. A handoff acceptance certificate or return-of-sources obligation may be identical bytes on both sides. Contract administration reads the clause and obligation; this row reads the document's attachment to exact creative package members. Neither is allowed to infer the other's facts.

`logistics` owns custody and movement of goods, with its strongest recognition built from consignor, consignee, carrier, goods, place and acknowledgement roles. A courier POD can show that an SSD arrived. It cannot show what bytes were on the SSD or whether the recipient accepted the creative work. Conversely, a manifest and email acceptance do not establish carrier custody. A source-media custody receipt that names both the device and its creative members can support both schemas.

## Node test, argued in full

### 1. Detection signals

The creative default already detects working-to-export lineage and a manifest-like note. The narrower row adds a relational chain with independently observable joins:

1. A manifest enumerates exact member filenames and preferably byte identity evidence such as checksums or sizes.
2. A sender/recipient record cites the package, transfer identifier, manifest revision, or member names.
3. A receiver-side event records receipt or artifact-specific acceptance, not merely a sender's claim of upload.
4. A rights schedule, third-party-asset disclosure, retention/deletion instruction, source return term, or custody record maps back to those members.

No single token is decisive. The joins are decisive because they survive deletion of generic vocabulary. Delete “final,” “delivery,” “client,” and every extension from a real fixture; the manifest/member match, transfer identifier, receiver acknowledgement, and rights mapping remain. Perform the same deletion on `logo_FINAL.ai` beside a generic WeTransfer email; nothing remains that distinguishes handoff from review or backup.

The strict three-link lifecycle is why the row survives. If Joseph relaxes it to package plus acceptance, the remaining evidence is already owned by the schema default and `creative.client-engagement`, and refusal becomes the correct result.

### 2. Recommended dimensions

There is no distinguishing dimension order. The creative schema declares no fields, so this placeholder must serialize `dimension_order: []`. The prose recommendation, contingent on later adoption of the schema's existing canonical proposals, is the creative default: project, then delivered stage, then artifact type, with client only where a corpus genuinely contains multiple clients. This row does not mint package, recipient, transfer, rights, or custody dimensions.

This leg therefore fails to distinguish the node. That is acceptable because one node-test leg is sufficient, but it is important negative evidence. The package revision and transfer identifier are excellent join evidence and retrieval metadata; they are not automatically good folder levels. Dates are particularly unsafe because creation, upload, receipt, acceptance, rights-effective, expiry, publication, return and deletion are separate clocks. `time_first` is false.

### 3. Privacy and custody rules

The creative schema is already potentially sensitive, so generic commercial confidentiality does not distinguish the row. The narrower difference is concentration and transfer apparatus. A handoff bundle often combines unpublished masters, editable sources, licensed fonts, stock media, model/property releases, signatures, personal recipient contacts, transfer URLs, passwords or opening instructions, and contractual rights schedules. It may expose third-party material that is absent from the public final export.

The custody tail adds a real constraint: a receiver can possess bytes without owning copyright; a source drive can be lent with a return duty; credentials can be shared for a limited transfer and should not become folder names or model context; an archive copy can be required to be deleted after acceptance. The row does not author a handling class, but it must prevent the benign appearance of a flattened export from weakening protection for the package around it. This is a modest privacy-leg distinction rather than the primary basis for survival.

## Concrete files: first eight core fixtures

### 1. `Northwind_Final_Artwork_Delivery_Manifest_v2.xlsx`

- `source_type`: `spreadsheet`; likely `.xlsx` or `.csv`.
- Inside: one row per exact output, including filename, format, colour space, dimensions or channel, intended use, checksum or size, package revision, sender and recipient.
- Observations: table labels and matching neighbouring members. The checksum is direct byte-identity evidence only after the records name the same object.
- Legal facts: none from this template because `creative` declares no fields.
- Must remain unknown: whether intended use grants legal permission; whether the recipient is the commissioning client; whether adjacent unlisted exports belong.
- Residual: `Independent Records` if the members or receiver chain cannot be established.

- Must remain unknown: creative acceptance, legal acceptance, payment approval, authority of the sender.

The discriminant is not that this row is creative by name; it is that this row can trace exact exported media through the receiver event and rights/custody tail.

### `final_exports/` and `Delivery.zip`

## Reciprocal boundaries

### `creative.client-engagement`

From this row toward the neighbour: a brief, scope, review round, commissioner feedback, or general approval belongs to the engagement until exact delivered members connect to receiver-side receipt/acceptance and rights/custody. From the neighbour toward this row: a delivery manifest and acceptance email remain engagement evidence because they close the commissioner-maker lifecycle, but this row may additionally activate for a printer, publisher, broadcaster, developer, archive, or successor agency that receives files without being the commissioning client. Same competing bytes: `Northwind_Final_Artwork_Delivery_Manifest_v2.xlsx` and `Northwind_Identity_Master_R3_acceptance-signed.pdf`.

The practical rule is altitude. Client engagement is the whole commissioned relationship. Deliverable handoff is the closure dossier for one package revision. The latter must not swallow briefs, concept decks, review comments, invoices, or status records merely because they share a job reference.

### `business_operations.project-delivery`

From this row toward the neighbour: closure, sponsor approval, risk sign-off, lessons learned and benefits review stay project-delivery when the governed effort is the anchor. From project-delivery toward this row: a deliverable register becomes handoff evidence only when rows identify creative members and connect to a receiver plus rights or custody. Same competing bytes: an acceptance certificate can be both; `Project Phoenix Closure and Benefits Review.pdf` is the falsifier that stays business-only.

The word deliverable is never the seam. It is a generic work-product value. The seam is governance apparatus versus semantic/byte custody of an enumerated creative package.

### `business_operations.contract-administration`

From this row toward the neighbour: clause references, obligation owners, notice periods, remedies, renewals and formal variations belong to contract administration. From contract administration toward this row: an acceptance or custody instrument joins handoff only through exact package/member references. Same competing bytes: `Northwind_Identity_Master_R3_acceptance-signed.pdf` and the source custody receipt.

An instrument can support both schemas without one stealing the other's facts. This row never decides enforceability or satisfaction of a clause. Contract administration never infers that the listed bytes were readable, complete, or creatively approved unless its evidence says so.

### `creative.licensing-rights`

From this row toward the neighbour: licences, releases, clearances, expiry and ownership records remain rights evidence even if no handoff ever occurred. From rights toward this row: a rights schedule joins handoff only when it maps to named package members or a manifest revision. Same bytes: `Northwind_Usage_and_Third-Party_Assets_Schedule.pdf` can support both; `StockPhoto_84721_License.pdf` supports rights alone.

This boundary prevents rights/custody from becoming padding. The third lifecycle link must be relational: it must answer which delivered member is governed or who holds which source, not merely put a licence PDF in the same folder.

### `logistics.shipment`

From this row toward logistics: carrier, tracking, route, delivery address and proof of physical arrival belong to shipment. From logistics toward this row: physical custody joins creative only when the medium or parcel maps to enumerated creative members and a creative transfer condition. Same competing bytes: `Courier POD - SSD SN-A1B2C3.pdf` stays logistics; `Source_Files_Custody_Receipt_Studio-to-Northwind.pdf` can support both.

The byte-versus-medium distinction is essential. A hard drive can arrive empty, encrypted, corrupted, or holding a different revision. Carrier POD proves parcel arrival; manifest/member checks prove semantic package identity; acceptance proves a receiver event; custody terms prove what should happen afterward.

## Neighbours considered without edges

- `career`: a consultant's work-product delivery can have the same sender/receiver and acceptance shape, but this row is gated by creative artifact lineage and package members. The mandated neighbour was considered; no direct edge was emitted because the precise roster node would be `career.consulting-client-engagement`, and `creative.client-engagement` already carries that cross-domain seam. Adding a second edge here would broaden the node from creative handoff to generic professional-services delivery.
- `code`: release bundles, build artifacts, checksums, SBOMs and deployment acceptance resemble this lifecycle. They remain code when repository/build/release structure is the anchor. No edge is emitted because the assigned charge focused on project, contract, rights and logistics boundaries, and a creative source package without repository structure does not compete on the same evidence.
- `photos`: exported galleries, selects, proofs and download links can be delivered. Capture metadata and personal photo collections stay photos; commissioned making and recipient handoff stay creative. No edge is emitted because a photo extension or gallery alone is explicitly never-alone and the schema-level creative/photos seam already carries the distinction.
- `finance`: final invoices and payment receipts may coexist and therefore receive `also_holds_with`, not a collision. Money does not prove package receipt or acceptance.
- `legal`: signed rights, acceptance and custody instruments may legally also hold, so the relation is coexistence, not mutex. This row disclaims legal effect.

## Fields and proposals

`fields` is exactly `[]`. `proposed_fields` is exactly `[]`. The row reuses the creative schema's pending concepts only in prose and does not duplicate them. Package revision, transfer identifier, recipient, acceptance status, rights scope and custody status are plausible searchable facts, but this template is not authorised to add schema fields.

The recipient-role gap is real. The commissioning `client` can differ from the printer, publisher, broadcaster, developer, archive, escrow holder or downstream agency receiving the package. The JSON records that difference in `role_split` without minting a key. A recipient is also a poor destination candidate: contact names and transfer addresses may be personal, and one package may pass through several recipients.

The manifest member list and checksum are evidence, not folder dimensions. Checksums are universal byte-identity observations; package IDs are freely generated values; neither licenses a new creative field here.

## Residual routing

- `Independent Records`: a durable readme, manifest, acceptance note or transmittal with no recoverable group.
- `Receipts and Confirmations`: a transfer receipt, download event, courier POD or acknowledgement proving only a transaction.
- `Review Later`: partial package evidence where the member, receiver, acceptance, or rights/custody join remains unresolved.
- `Protected Records`: isolated signed acceptance, licences, releases, credentials, private transfer details or custody forms.
- `Unsupported or Encrypted`: an opaque or password-protected handoff archive whose members were not inspected.

The residuals are not weaker versions of this template. They are the honest destinations after the row declines to fire.

## NEEDS-JOSEPH

**NJ-CDH-1 — strict lifecycle threshold.** Choose between: (a) require all three substantive links—enumerated package, receipt or exact-version acceptance, and mapped rights or custody—as this row does; (b) allow package plus acceptance, making rights/custody optional; or (c) refuse the row and let the creative default plus `creative.client-engagement` own all handoff evidence. Option (a) best survives the node test. Option (b) has higher recall but likely duplicates existing activation. Option (c) is preferable to a vague row.

**NJ-CDH-2 — recipient role.** Choose between: (a) a canonical non-destination `recipient` fact shared across delivery domains; (b) a role-qualified generic organisation relation; or (c) recognition-only, as this row currently records. Reusing `client` is rejected because recipient and commissioner are often different roles.

**NJ-CDH-3 — physical media overlap.** Choose whether a source-drive custody receipt should activate both creative handoff and `logistics.shipment`, or whether logistics retains the carrier record while creative holds only a semantic package reference. The proposed reciprocal seam is physical custody versus enumerated byte/package identity; an explicit cross-group link may be cleaner than dual membership for the POD itself.

## Final consistency check

The retained node is not a file-transfer format, not “delivered” as a stage, not approval vocabulary, and not terminal project state. It activates only from a relational lifecycle whose joins can be cited: exact members, receiver event, and mapped rights or custody. The JSON and memo both declare no fields or proposed fields, no dimensions, placeholder launch, potentially sensitive handling posture, the same five collision edges, the same residual set, and the same three NEEDS-JOSEPH items. If NJ-CDH-1 rejects the strict lifecycle, the honest next action is refusal rather than dilution.

**End verdict: KEEP narrowly; otherwise REFUSE if the package → acceptance → rights/custody chain is not ratified.**
