## The two reversals, stated plainly

The deepening addendum requires that a disagreement with the draft be stated, not silently
performed. Two of the gist memo's three grounds are withdrawn.

**Reversal 1 — the SCALE argument is withdrawn.** The gist memo's leading claim was that *"scale
changes the OBJECT"* and that *"the anchor changes"*. The first half is rhetoric standing in for
evidence: a line drawn on how many files a folder holds would need a threshold, and `_CONTRACT`
rule 3 forbids inventing one. The second half is worse than that — the deepened schema anchor
addresses **this row by name** and pre-empts it:

> *"A row whose entire life is one job at one address — `construction_property.trade-job`,
> `construction_property.construction-project` — may honestly put the instruction first, because
> property-first would produce exactly the "meaningless one-child levels" the validator rejects.
> **Reversing is not a difference that earns a node**; it is one of the things a template is *for*,
> and a sibling claiming a node on the reversal alone has claimed nothing."*

The gist memo led with the reversal. It has claimed nothing. A **new** `never_alone` entry was added
this pass making the point enforceable rather than editorial: *the SCALE of the surrounding corpus*
is now never-alone evidence on this row, alongside the address and the person's name.

**Reversal 2 — leg 3 (privacy) is downgraded from "the strongest of the three" to FAILING.** The
gist memo wrote: *"The privacy posture changes … That is the third ground, and it is the strongest of
the three."* Measured against the **deepened** anchor, that is false. The anchor already sets exactly
these grounds — *"The material names a real person's home and who is in it"* and *"The exposed party
is usually not the user, and cannot consent"* — as the schema default **for all 27 siblings**. A
ground held by every sibling cannot distinguish one of them. What is genuinely peculiar to a trade's
corpus is **volume**, and volume is scale, which this row has just finished refusing to treat as
evidence. It will not be smuggled back in through the privacy leg. `sensitivity_why` now says so in
the JSON, so the finding is checkable and not merely confessed in a memo.

This is the same finding `construction_property.construction-project` made about its own leg 3, and
the two rows are now consistent.

**What was NOT reversed.** The gist memo's *second* ground — that the document population changes,
because most files here are machine-generated by field-service software and carry a captured customer
signature — is correct, survives, and is now the row's whole claim. It was the least emphasised of
the three and it was the only real one.

## The node test, all three legs, argued

CONNECTION.md §2: *"A **template** row exists only if its detection signals, recommended dimensions,
or privacy rules differ from its schema's default template."* Any one leg suffices. The anchor states
the default so the test is checkable rather than rhetorical:

> **`property` or site → `instruction` (the job, the letting, the scheme, the block) → document
> function**, with a period level only where the situation *genuinely cycles*. **Not time-first.**

And it states the trap:

> *`variation`, `snagging`, `dilapidations`, `retention`, `preliminaries`, `certificate`, `drawing`,
> `schedule`, `survey`, `valuation` and `report` are **values of `work_type`**, not rows.*

`quotation`, `invoice`, `certificate` and `photograph` are values on that reading too. **A row cannot
be earned by holding a quote, an invoice, a certificate and some photographs**, which is precisely
the dispatch's second charge. Everything below is written knowing that.

### Leg 1 — detection signals. The only passing leg.

The question, put as sharply as it can be: **does any structure activate this row that does not
activate a sibling, the schema default, or another schema entirely?** After conceding everything
that belongs elsewhere, **one** does.

**The field-service work record.** A machine-generated PDF carrying, *together and in labelled
positions*: a job or reference number; a customer name-and-address block; a scheduled date; a
technician or engineer name; **a description of the work carried out**; **parts used with
quantities**; **labour time**; a total; and a **captured customer signature image** taken at the foot
at the point of performance.

The discriminator is not the signature. It is the **work-performed triple** — work carried out, parts
used, labour time — recorded against a **fixed property** and **signed for by the person who occupies
it**. Tested against every rival:

| Rival structure | Why it is not this one |
|---|---|
| The anchor's four schema-default structures — title block, measured-works table, the *to date, less previously certified* arithmetic, apportionment schedule | **None of the four is present on a job sheet**, and the work-performed triple is on none of the four. This is the test that matters most: the row is not recognised by the family's default detection method. |
| `construction-project`'s four surviving structures — the award sequence, the contract-particulars block, the programme, the completion-and-handover envelope | All four absent. A job is not constituted by an award, has no possession-and-completion pair, no programme, and no assembled handover pack. This is the answer to the scale charge, and it is **structural**: a difference in which documents exist, not in how many. |
| `materials-delivery`'s delivery note and proof-of-delivery capture | The closest internal rival, and it forced a **narrowing** this pass. Its discriminating shape is its own: *"a DELIVER-TO address that differs from the invoice-to address, line items with quantities delivered — often against a separate ordered column — and a received-by signature block"*, plus a discrepancy annotation. A delivery note records **goods received**; a job sheet records **labour performed**. Neither has the other's core. |
| `plant-hire`'s hire docket | Its anchor is *"an ASSET plus a HIRE PERIOD"* with an off-hire notice as the discriminating document. No asset identity, no hire period, no off-hire on a job sheet. |
| `final-account`'s application for payment | Cumulative arithmetic against what was previously certified. A job invoice is a snapshot; there is no cycle to be cumulative against. |
| `quote-estimate`'s priced offer | The sibling states the split itself: *"a validity period, an acceptance mechanism and no work carried out supports this row; a job or works reference, scheduled dates, labour and materials actually used, and site records support the job row."* Work **carried out** is the far side of that line by the sibling's own words. |
| `finance.household-property`'s improvement record | Its `Kitchen Remodel - Bright Plumbing - Invoice 7841.pdf` has *"line items describ[ing] installed plumbing work, labour and materials under one job reference"* — genuinely close. It has **no scheduled visit, no technician, no parts-used table and no signature capture**, and the holder is the owner. See the outbound fixture below. |
| `manufacturing.field-service-report` | The one rival **outside** the family, and the honest one: the same class of software produces both, byte-for-byte. Discriminator: a **serialised asset** with an equipment model and a service contract there, versus a **fixed property** here. |

**The honest weakness in leg 1, stated rather than hidden.** The work-performed triple selects the
*situation*; it does not on its own select the *schema*. What makes it construction rather than
manufacturing is that the work was performed **on a property**, and a property address is this
family's constitutional never-alone. So leg 1 rests on a **conjunction** — the work-performed triple
*plus* a property anchor — of which the second half can never fire by itself. That is a legitimate
conjunctive signal under CONNECTION.md §4 (never-alone constrains *alone*, not *in combination*), and
it is exactly the shape `legal.leases-agreements` uses. But it is thinner than the anchor's title
block, and the memo does not pretend otherwise.

**Verdict on leg 1: PASSES, on one structure.** It would fail on any weaker reading, and the JSON's
`open_question` names the condition under which R1c should conclude that it does.

### Leg 2 — recommended dimensions. Weak; recorded, not relied on.

`dimension_order` is `[]` **by binding contract** — a dimension may only branch on a field the same
entry's schema declares, and this schema declares none (`_CONTRACT` rules 10 and 15, PR-6). So the
recommendation is prose in `template.why`, and is: **client or property address → the job → document
function, with the function level usually flattened.**

Against the family default, that is **not** the licensed reversal. The anchor pre-licenses
`instruction`-before-`property` for this row and warns that the reversal earns nothing. This row's
recommendation puts **`client` first** — a level the family default does not contain at all. A trade's
corpus is a **list of customers**, and the customer is not the property: a landlord with four flats, a
letting agent, a facilities manager and a housing association are each one client across many
addresses, and a property-first tree scatters that relationship.

**And it is still not a passing leg**, for a reason internal to `00`. A client level above a one-off
domestic customer's single job is precisely *"use an author or organization merely as a collector"*
and produces *"meaningless one-child levels"* — both named validator prohibitions. So client-first is
right for repeat customers and wrong for one-off ones, and **no threshold may be written to separate
them** (`_CONTRACT` rule 3). A recommendation that is correct only half the time and cannot say which
half is information for a sibling author, not a node ground.

The `commercial-lease` method is instructive here and is why it works there and not here. That row
earned leg 1 by arguing what the family's `instruction` level **means**: *"The family's `instruction`
level assumes a commissioned piece of work with a start and an end; a tenancy is a relationship with
a term."* **This row is the paradigm case of the thing that argument contrasts with.** A trade job is
a commissioned piece of work with a start and an end, and it is the family's `instruction` level in
its purest form. It cannot earn a node by redefining a level it exemplifies. That the sibling's
argument runs *against* this row is the sharpest single finding of this pass, and it is stated
because the dispatch was right that it bears directly.

**Verdict on leg 2: differs from the default, and is nonetheless DISCLAIMED as a basis for the node.**

### Leg 3 — privacy rules. FAILS, reversing the gist memo.

Argued above under **Reversal 2**. The grounds are the schema's, held by all 27 siblings; the only
thing peculiar to this row is volume; volume is scale; scale is not evidence here or anywhere else on
this row. `site-health-safety` is the sibling that is genuinely stricter than the schema default, and
this row is not that row. No P7 handling class is assigned; that is P7's.

**Verdict on leg 3: identical to the schema default. Recorded as failing.**

### Overall

**Kept, on leg 1 alone.** One leg is all §2 requires, and claiming two or three would be the
unsourced confidence the brief forbids.

### Why this is not the `organisational-records` refusal

The dispatch is right that the comparison is the test, so it is drawn rather than asserted. That
refusal turned on a specific reduction:

> *"Its only candidate signal is an organisation name plus a document-type word, and an organisation
> name is constitutionally never-alone … A row whose entire support is never-alone evidence can never
> clear activation, so it would be a row that never fires."*

This row's candidate signal does **not** reduce that way. Strip its never-alone material — the
address, the customer's name, the trading name, the job number, the invoice shape, the trade
vocabulary, the EXIF, the folder name, and now the corpus scale — and what remains is a **document
structure**: work carried out, parts used, labour time, signed for at a property. A structure is not
a name. That is the whole difference between this row and the refusal, and between this row and
`compliance-certificate`, which was refused inside this very family because its candidate signal
reduced to *a document-type word plus an address* — **both halves never-alone**.

**The row is one concession away from that fate**, and the JSON says so: if R1c gives the work record
to `materials-delivery`, to `manufacturing.field-service-report`, or to `construction-project` as a
`work_type` value, then nothing structural is left, the row becomes the union of its siblings' papers,
and it should be refused with its coverage routed to the six residuals it already names. Naming the
falsifier is the point.

pass draws the line on **whether the contract machinery is documentary** — is there a
contract-particulars block, a certifying third party, a retention line? — because a line drawn on
size would need a threshold. **NJ-CP-JOB-3**, below.

## Grouping, and the firewall

Five accepted grouping reasons are in the JSON, and the reason a five-file job is a legitimate group
at all is `00`'s own sentence: *"The documents are content-incoherent but purpose-coherent."* A quote,
two photographs, a materials receipt, a certificate and an invoice share no vocabulary, no format and
no author. They share a customer and an address — and **that is a grouping reason, never an activation
proof**, because the address is never-alone.

**The firewall, and it is this row's most dangerous member: the customer export.** A field-service
application's archive whose manifest lists per-customer folders is evidence about a **portfolio** and
not evidence about any customer it names. Its fixture says so, *"the normal scan should never extract
archive contents to the filesystem"*, and *"A session should never be treated as proof of topic"*
kills the parallel inference from a bulk download.

**A second firewall added by this pass:** browse position. A file under a customer folder or a job
number is **browsing** there; the row that owns the file's own structure is what **activates**.
CONNECTION.md §3 makes the folder parent a dimension and not a schema id, and a job folder is the most
persuasive false context this row will ever meet — which is exactly the discipline
`construction-project` had to learn as a branch root. It is now a `never_alone` entry rather than a
paragraph in a memo.

**And no group at all is valid.** A single materials receipt from a trade counter is an isolated
transactional document and goes to Receipts and Confirmations. On a real trade's drive, that is the
most common outcome by a wide margin.
