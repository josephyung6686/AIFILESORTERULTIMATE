# Research memo — `creative.post-production`

**Depth: J-DEPTH.** Deepened with concrete lifecycle fixtures and boundary analysis.

## Verdict

Keep the row as a non-refused, placeholder `creative` template. It earns distinctness through a recognizable post-production lifecycle and evidence bundle, not through a new schema, extension list, or folder path. The creative schema is fieldless under J-IND, so `fields` and `dimension_order` remain empty. Universal facts (`file_type`, `creation_date`, `language`, `version_family`, `sensitivity_status`) may still be recorded; they are not post-production fields.

The lifecycle is: ingest/media manifest → editable project and timeline → review/time-coded feedback → grade and/or mix → master/export → QC or approval. A single `.mov`, `.prproj`, `FINAL` suffix, or “approved” email does not activate it. Activation needs production context and a recoverable relationship among the stages. A sparse cut can join a group without receiving project or stage facts from its neighbours.

## Proposed fields

None. `project`, `stage`, `artifact_type`, `work_type` and `client` are existing or pending creative concepts, but this row must not mint or copy them while the creative schema remains fieldless. `version_family` is universal metadata, not a post-production field. A later schema decision may make lifecycle stages or artifact types values under a project; it should not turn this row into a schema.

## Neighbours considered without edges

- `career`: post-production files can appear in portfolios or job applications, but application purpose evidence belongs to career; a production lifecycle is not inferred from a portfolio export.
- `code`: “render”, “build”, “release”, project files and version tokens occur in software repositories. Repository markers and code-structured evidence separate the collision; no edge is authored here because this row does not share a decisive activation fixture.
- `photos`: camera originals and phone recordings can become editorial source media, but EXIF and a media extension alone do not show editing. The lone-phone fixture is `also_schema: photos` as a possible co-holding case, not a post-production collision.
- `creative.client-engagement`: a client review or approval may co-occur, but this row’s discriminator is the production assembly lifecycle. The engagement row owns commissioner-maker evidence; this row owns the ingest-to-master evidence.
- `creative.revision-round`: revision tokens and feedback are universal/group evidence. They become post-production evidence only when tied to a cut/timeline and production lifecycle; the refused revision row remains the owner of no template activation.
- `creative.shoot-day-media`: camera originals and dailies are upstream source media. A manifest can be adjacent to both, but shoot-day capture evidence does not prove editorial assembly.

## NEEDS-JOSEPH

Decide later whether the lifecycle should be a named UI/graph view or remain a template applicability record. Recommended: keep the row, leave it fieldless and dimensionless, and represent stage transitions through evidence-backed P9 relationships plus user-confirmed `stage`/`work_type` values if the creative schema is ratified. Do not claim causal order from filesystem timestamps, filename numbering, or a folder called `Post`.

## Ending claims

- This row is not a schema per application, codec, work type, or file extension.
- It does not authorize folder paths or project facts on neighbouring files.
- It does not treat `FINAL`, `approved`, or a master filename as approval.
- It recognizes a bounded post-production evidence lifecycle and otherwise falls through safely.
